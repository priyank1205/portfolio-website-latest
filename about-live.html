A rehearsal before the drill that counts.

A teenager props a phone up, steps back and is scored by its camera, in an event a parent paid for. So onboarding is a rehearsal on the exact screen the scored test uses: the first time it counts is the second time they have seen it.

The labels were the feature.

The share export proved hard to build, and CEDA proposed exporting the heatmap alone. That would have removed what made the image readable outside the portal. I worked it through with the developer until we could build it. Almost two years after handover, CEDA’s March 2026 posts use it.

Build with curiosity

A task worth returning to.

Research by playing.

At Getmega I watched players stream on YouTube and Twitch, followed how they moved between products, and played across real-money platforms myself. Players went where the tournaments were good and the opponents beatable. The answer was a loyalty programme of gameplay-tied tasks, and I designed it end to end.
