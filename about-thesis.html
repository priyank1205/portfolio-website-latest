Hi, I’m Priyank.

I’m a senior product designer in Bengaluru. I design consumer apps, internal tools, dashboards and AI-powered products for startups across stages.

I chose interaction design because it sits at the intersection of psychology and technology, two lifelong curiosities. What excites me is understanding how people think, what businesses need and where technology is headed, then connecting those dots into products people enjoy using.

At IIT Guwahati I tried graphic design, 3D and animation before I found that. Some of it stayed: I still make things for the fun of it, and I still play.

Design with empathy

Start from where people are standing.

Sometimes that is literal: a phone propped up across a room, or a chart read far from where it was made. More often it is the moment someone trusts a screen with something that matters: paying, being scored, reading a number they will repeat to someone else.

I design for that moment first, and then for every state around it.

Think like a product owner

Care about what ships, and why.

A design is only as good as the version that reaches people. I work straight with the people who decide, ask what the business needs, and keep the detail that carries the meaning, even when it is the hard one to build.

The best design decisions rarely come from pixels alone.

Build with curiosity

Learn by playing, then by building.

To understand a product, I use it, watch other people use it, and try its rivals. Lately that curiosity runs straight into code: prototypes become products faster, and designers become builders.

So I build small tools for myself, in the evenings, end to end.
