Hi, I’m Priyank.

I’m a senior product designer in Bengaluru. I design consumer apps, internal tools, dashboards and AI-powered products for startups across stages.

I chose interaction design because it sits at the intersection of psychology and technology, two lifelong curiosities. What excites me is understanding how people think, what businesses need and where technology is headed, then connecting those dots into products people enjoy using.

Lately that means AI-native building: prototypes become products faster, and designers become builders.

Design with empathyKhiladiPro

A rehearsal before the drill that counts.

A teenager props a phone up, steps back and is scored by its camera, in an event a parent paid for. If onboarding failed, the drill could not be scored. So I designed onboarding as a rehearsal on the exact screen the scored test uses: the first time it counts is the second time they have seen it.

Think like a product ownerSEDP

The labels were the feature.

The share export proved hard to build, and CEDA proposed exporting the heatmap alone. That would have removed what made the image readable outside the portal. I researched how it could be built and worked it through with the developer until we could. Almost two years after handover, CEDA’s March 2026 posts use it.

Build with curiosityGetmega

Research by playing.

At Getmega I watched players stream on YouTube and Twitch, followed how they moved between products, and played across real-money platforms myself. Players went where the tournaments were good and the opponents beatable. The product team answered with a loyalty programme of gameplay-tied tasks, and I designed it end to end.
