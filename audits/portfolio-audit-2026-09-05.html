Turn strong execution evidence into a senior product-design case.

Your portfolio already demonstrates independent delivery and considered craft. The highest-value work is making senior judgment, collaboration, and trustworthy evidence easier to see.

4 versions reviewed Khiladipro + SEDP Desktop + mobile checks Recruiter + founder audiences

The central diagnosis

The site currently makes a stronger case for capable freelance execution than for a midweight-to-senior product designer operating inside a product team. The senior material is present, especially in Getmega, but a reader has to assemble that argument themselves.

Recommended territory

A hands-on product designer who makes complex workflows easier to understand and carries them through delivery with small teams.

What to fix first

Correct trust-breaking inconsistencies before adding process diagrams, rewriting pages, or polishing visual details.

01

Correct the evidence

Draft copy, inconsistent data, conflicting setup guidance, incorrect client naming, and unsupported claims can make a careful reviewer doubt the rest of the story.

02

Show senior judgment

Add genuine decision episodes: what you learned, the alternatives considered, the constraint, the collaborator, and the resulting choice.

03

Make fit immediate

Position around complex workflows and small-team delivery. State full-time and contract availability without creating separate identities.

One identity, two ways to work together

“End-to-end,” “0→1 & growth stage,” “mobile, web, and dashboards,” and “AI-native workflows” establish breadth, but they do not tell a recruiter or founder why to choose you.

Positioning direction: I make complex products easier to understand and use. I work with small teams on consumer apps and data tools, from shaping the experience to the details that ship.

Use “Senior Product Designer” if it aligns with your resume and application materials. Keep “midweight-to-senior” as an internal search range, not public positioning.

What each audience needs

Recruiters and hiring managers: level, recent work, product ownership, collaboration, location, employment availability, and a readable resume.

Founders: the problems you take on, what you own, how you work with a team, what gets delivered, and how to start.

Keep one portfolio. Offer clear routes such as “Discuss a role” and “Discuss a project,” both leading to the same inbox.

Use two real decision episodes per flagship: input → alternatives → constraint → choice → collaboration → evidence. A small, honest revision is stronger than a reconstructed process diagram or fabricated research artifact.

Default reading path

  1. Problem, role, engagement type, duration, team, and strongest defensible result.
  2. Three consequential decisions with legible evidence beside each.
  3. Observed outcome, stated limits, and one specific reflection.

Keep registration proof, core before/after evidence, physical setup, and published-use examples out of disclosures. Use disclosures for extra screens, process depth, and extended measurement plans.

Portfolio composition

Khiladipro: complex web-to-app journey, paid entry, physical setup, camera feedback.

SEDP: information architecture, data comprehension, comparison, and distribution.

Getmega: sustained product ownership, iteration through change, engineering partnership, and team contribution.

Lead with these three. Use Mega Poker and Ecometer & Agrimarket as targeted supporting evidence.

Rebalance visual attention

The redesigns look more coherent than the originals. Preserve that, then move emphasis from presentation framing to evidence inspection.

A

Show work sooner

The homepage opening prioritizes introduction, live status, shortcuts, and contact before a strong work example. Shorten this sequence.

B

Expose key facts earlier

On the tested mobile views, role/team facts began more than 1,000 pixels down the redesigned case studies. Bring a compact summary above or immediately after the hero.

C

Use inspectable artifacts

Pair one overview image with close, annotated crops. Whole desktop screens compressed into narrow columns do not prove control hierarchy, labels, or data context.

Keep the redesigns’ evidence discipline: a rehire establishes client confidence; a social post establishes use of a feature; neither alone establishes conversion, usability, or audience growth.

Your real differentiators are specific

  • A phone communicating to someone three metres away.
  • Two charts that look comparable but use different years, filters, or units.
  • A financial input whose value can be hidden by a user’s thumb.
  • A product component surviving thirteen different states.

Use these situations to establish your point of view. Generic phrases about empathy, ownership, clarity, or business goals are useful only when attached to evidence.

AI positioning needs an artifact

Khiladipro is an AI-powered product, but that does not itself demonstrate your AI-assisted building workflow. The YouTube extension shows builder curiosity but is currently WIP and mostly links to a repository.

If AI-assisted building remains prominent, show a working demo, a real user problem, one iteration, your contribution, and the limits of the implementation. Until then, keep it as supporting evidence rather than a primary hiring claim.

Version-by-version guidance

Use the redesigned studies as the public foundation. Mine the originals for specificity and evidence; do not restore their full structures.

Khiladipro · Original

Keep the physical context. Remove the repeated setup.

Keep: the explicit freelance role, eight-week constraint, audience questions, mobile parent-reassurance tradeoff, detailed onboarding states, and the visible follow-up engagement.

Change: hero, short version, statistics, brief, constraints, and experience map repeat too much context. Transfer the strongest evidence into the redesign. Correct the draft artwork, phone-placement conflict, unsupported traffic/superlative claims, and “07 chapters” mismatch.

Khiladipro · Redesign

Keep the structure. Put the proof in the default path.

Keep: conversion → activation → participation, concise ownership, “first scoreable drill” as activation, and bounded rehire language.

Change: lead with sole freelance designer, eight-week first engagement, four-person team, and verified rehire. Elevate the three-metre setup sequence, show registration proof by default, and add a real decision changed by stakeholder or technical feedback.

SEDP · Original

Keep the concrete evidence. Qualify the claims.

Keep: old-interface problems, detailed crops, distribution rationale, and a visible social-use example.

Change: remove unsupported claims about audience size and speed; stop presenting no-scroll as universally better; explain the redesign of an existing comparison workflow; correct share-map data and remove browser/Figma chrome.

SEDP · Redesign

It is the strongest structure. Make interpretation tangible.

Keep: first use → compare → share, the distinction between published use and growth, and the attention to different years, units, filters, and comprehension.

Change: choose one representative reader task, use matched before/after crops, show a comparison state with relevant context, surface a verified published export, and establish the status of touch, keyboard, small-screen, and missing-data handling.

Getmega should be featured

It is your strongest proof of sustained product-team experience: two years, pivots, daily leadership reviews, product/engineering coordination, and contribution as the design team grew. Build it around one anchor story, not an inventory of every flow.

The 15k → 25k DAU figure is appropriately labeled a team outcome, but the attribution needs a time window, source context, and careful causal wording.

Supporting work needs a clearer job

Mega Poker: retain transparent money handling and constrained redesign; balance business retention language with user choice, clarity, and recovery.

Ecometer & Agrimarket: either add two specific decisions and a shared-system example, or present it as a supporting gallery linked from SEDP.

About: replace generic principles with examples of scoping, reviewing, and collaborating.

Prioritized fix plan

Estimated effort assumes the necessary source assets and project records are available. It is planning guidance, not a delivery commitment.

Correct factual and visual contradictions P0 · trust

Fix draft imagery, chart values, setup guidance, CEDA naming, quote versions, unsupported novelty claims, and unclear engagement scope.

Recover two real decision episodes per flagship P1 · seniority

Bring in actual early/later artifacts, stakeholder feedback, engineering compromises, and any post-launch learning.

Rewrite the homepage around specific value P1 · fit

State the product problems you solve and make full-time and contract availability explicit.

Feature Khiladipro, SEDP, and Getmega P1 · composition

Give each project a different proof role so the portfolio demonstrates breadth without repeating itself.

Move essential proof into the skim path P1 · scanability

Role, duration, result, and key artifacts should be visible before disclosures or external prototype links.

Standardize visual presentation P2 · presentation

Use consistent personal navigation, shorter openings, inspectable crops, and a quieter interaction layer.

Test the revised story with target readers P2 · validation

Ask recruiters, design managers, and founders what they believe you do, what evidence they trust, and what still blocks a conversation.

Evidence to recover before rewriting

  • Exact dates and scope for each engagement, including the Khiladipro follow-up.
  • Final design versus shipped-product inventory.
  • Early and later artifacts for selected decisions.
  • Source of audience assumptions and constraints.
  • Engineering or stakeholder compromises and their impact.
  • Original testimonial wording and permission status.

How to assess the revision

  • Can a recruiter identify the role, level, availability, and strongest work quickly?
  • Can a design manager identify one consequential decision and why it was made?
  • Can a founder tell what they would hire you to do and how an engagement begins?
  • Can a mobile reader inspect the artifacts without opening every disclosure?
