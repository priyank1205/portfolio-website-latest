## 4. Reading paths and evidence handling

**Recruiter, 60–90 seconds:** S1 scope (10–15 s) → S2 W1/W2/W3 and one conclusion on amount, choice and status (25–30 s) → S3 D1/D2 and balance-destination distinction (10–15 s) → S4 G1/G2 and information/density tradeoff (15–20 s) → S5 delivery boundary (5–10 s). The summary names self-transfer; its complete supporting cluster remains in the main path. This is a scanning route, not a requirement to inspect every image.

**Design manager, 6–8 minutes:** Follow every section in order: inspect the two S2 clusters and choice asymmetry; compare deposit components and offer conditions; inspect the tournament comparison, filters and details; finish with delivery status. Show supporting artifacts openly. Expanders may hold recovered scope notes, not core evidence or corrections.

**Builder rules:**

- Internal labels: **FACT—artifact**, **FACT—reported**, **INFERENCE**, **MISSING**. Use plain attribution/provenance in final captions, not audit labels everywhere.
- No existing design export becomes a production capture through captioning. Original reference, design export, variant, and later correction must remain distinguishable.
- Use short artifact-led captions and at most three callouts per primary group. Keep main figures legible and individually inspectable; no new simulated product behaviour.
- Corrective disclosures belong next to the affected figure. General outcome limits appear once in S5.
- Do not create missing rationale, alternatives, collaboration, research or outcomes to fill a template. Missing evidence is not a missing chapter.
