03

The premium grammar

Eleven rules, each backed by the measurements above. These are the portable principles — independent of any one site's palette.

1 Type

Four font sizes

Median across the nine is four. Gavin's case studies run on one. Jenny and Jakub run whole sites on two.

Hierarchy from weight, colour and vertical space. Size is the blunt instrument you reach for when you haven't built the other three.

2 Surfaces

Hairlines, not shadows

Five of nine have zero box-shadows. Separation: 1–1.5px hairline at 8–12% opacity, or step the background value.

When shadows appear: multiple layers, low opacity — and always paired with a hairline as layer one.

3 Colour

An alpha ramp, not a grey list

Twelve solid greys for backgrounds/text; twelve matching rgba(…,x) steps for borders, dividers, hover fills, overlays.

Solid greys break over images. Alpha greys never do.

4 Colour

Never pure

#FDFDFC · #F1F1EE · #21201C · #1A1A1A — seven of nine avoid pure white and black. Greys are warm, a point or two toward yellow.

Jenny's pure black is the exception she earns: no surfaces for black to sit behind.

5 Type

Line height & tracking move inversely to size

72px display → lh 1.0, tracking −2.5%. 12px meta → lh 1.5, tracking +0.7px.

Large: tight leading, negative tracking. Small: open leading, positive tracking. This single relationship is most of professional typography.

6 Motion

One easing, one duration, colour only

cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1) on Emil, Brian, Jenny, Daniel. Durations cluster 0.15–0.25s. Nothing bounces. Nothing overshoots.

Transition colour properties and nothing else — or, like Paco, one property on the entire site.

7 Motion

One hover gesture, used everywhere

Tammy: scale(1.05). Paco: underline colour. Emil: text colour. Jakub & Jenny: nothing.

Pick one and do not invent a second.

8 Interaction

Contextual cursors beat custom cursors

Rauno swaps cursor to match what the element does — ew-resize, move, grabbing, copy, none.

Communicates rather than decorates, costs one CSS class, never fights the OS.

9 Media

Media is a system, not a collection

One asset per section, named after the section (Gavin, Daniel). Exported at display width. MP4 + autoplay loop muted playsinline + IntersectionObserver. Never GIF.

Compose, do not float — crop, bleed off an edge, sit in a soft panel.

10 Type

One typeface or the system stack — nothing in between

Paid face: Daniel (GT Standard), Paco (Söhne), Jenny (Neue Haas + Swear), Rauno (custom X). System: Jakub, Gavin. Inter: Brian.

Nobody reaches for a free display serif. One paid grotesk at two weights, or ui-sans-serif — both read as considered.

11 Personality

One contained moment of personality

Daniel's shader · Jenny's 72px logotype · Rauno's crosshair · Tammy's rotating line · Jakub's 2077 · Gavin's avatar.

Exactly one per site, in a named container, on a page otherwise completely sober. Distributed whimsy reads as noise; concentrated whimsy reads as confidence.

The three worst offenders

OFFENDER 01 · 43 FONT SIZES

Forty-three. Almost certainly the single largest reason the site does not feel as tight as the four you like. Values like 0.97rem, 0.94rem, 0.92rem, 0.9rem, 0.86rem are all present — no reader can perceive the difference; they just register as inconsistency.

OFFENDER 02 · SHADOWS AT 38% AND 50%

0 14px 34px rgba(0,0,0,0.38) and 0 24px 80px rgba(0,0,0,0.5). Nothing in the reference class goes above 12%. On a dark ground these read as grey haze rather than elevation.

OFFENDER 03 · SEVEN EASINGS INCLUDING OVERSHOOT

05

What to change

Fifteen moves ordered by ratio of perceived quality gained to work required — plus what not to do.

Tier 1Half a day · biggest visible jump
1
Collapse to a six-step type scale.Star

Compresses cleanly from 43 while keeping the dark, mono-inflected character:

/* page title, once per page */ --text-display: clamp(2rem, 4vw, 2.75rem); --text-title: 1.375rem; /* 22px section & card titles */ --text-lead: 1.0625rem; /* 17px intro paragraphs */ --text-body: 0.9375rem; /* 15px everything readable */ --text-small: 0.8125rem; /* 13px captions, meta */ --text-micro: 0.6875rem; /* 11px mono labels, eyebrows*/

Then grep -oE 'font-size: *[^;]+' assets/css/*.css and rewrite every hit to one of the six. Nothing else on this list will move the needle as much.

2
Replace both shadows with a layered ladder.Star

On dark, near-black + hairline does the work:

--shadow-border: 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.08); --shadow-sm: var(--shadow-border), 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.24); --shadow-md: var(--shadow-border), 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.20), 0 8px 16px -6px rgba(0,0,0,0.24); --shadow-lg: var(--shadow-border), 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.16), 0 12px 24px -8px rgba(0,0,0,0.28), 0 24px 48px -16px rgba(0,0,0,0.24);

Then delete 0 14px 34px rgba(0,0,0,0.38) and 0 24px 80px rgba(0,0,0,0.5).

3
One easing, two durations.Star
--ease: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1); --dur-fast: 0.15s; /* colour, opacity, borders */ --dur-slow: 0.3s; /* layout, reveals */

Delete both overshoot curves outright. Your existing cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.6, 0.2, 1) is a fine house curve if you prefer it — but keep only one.

4
Prefer colour transitions to transform transitions.

Audit every transition animating transform, scale or translate on hover. Would background-color or border-color do the job? Keep one transform hover in the whole site, at most.

5
Collapse radii to four.

Keep 2px (inline chips) · 10px (small surfaces) · 14px (cards, existing --radius) · 999px (pills). Delete 1, 6, 8, 9, 12, 16.

Tier 2A day · fixes the “almost right” feeling

Soft panel a step off the page background, crop, bleed off one edge. Stop centring full device mockups in empty space.

You get exactly one. Strongest candidate given your positioning: an operable demo of a real interaction you shipped, in the hero or top of the flagship — Daniel's shader, Ian's game. Not a scroll animation. Something the reader can grab.

15
Consider buying one typeface.

Inter is genuinely fine — Brian Lovin ships it. But GT Standard, Söhne and Neue Haas Grotesk do real work on three of the sites you liked. A single licensed grotesk at two weights is probably the cheapest step-change in perceived quality. Or go the other way to ui-sans-serif, system-ui like Gavin and Jakub. The one thing not to do is stack multiple free display faces.

Do not do
  • Do not add a custom cursor blob. Use contextual cursor values.
  • Do not add scroll-jacking, parallax or reveal-on-scroll curtains. None of the nine has any.
  • Do not add a second hover gesture.
  • Do not add gradients, glows or mesh backgrounds.
  • Do not use any easing curve that overshoots.
  • Do not raise a shadow above about 12% on any single layer.
Start here if you only do three things

Six type sizes. Layered shadows under 12%. One easing with zero overshoot.

Tier 1 items 1–3 are half a day and account for most of the gap. Everything else is refinement once those land.
